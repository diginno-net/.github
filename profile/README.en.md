Diginno is a Hanoi-based implementation studio building real operating systems with **Lark (LarkBase)**, **n8n**, system integrations, and AI. We move clients from manual spreadsheets to automated systems, and from fragmented data to real-time dashboards — prioritizing what runs in production over what looks good in a demo.

## What we build

| Area | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Lark / LarkBase systems** | CRM · HR, attendance & payroll · warehouse operations & POD · finance & e-invoicing · approvals · KPI/commission — as a company's internal operating system |
| **n8n automation** | Hundreds of production workflows that sync systems, remove manual work, and enforce data quality |
| **Integrations** | LarkSuite · Pancake · TikTok Shop · Shopee · KiotViet · NhanhVN · MISA · payment gateways · custom APIs |
| **Data & reporting** | PostgreSQL/DWH, ETL, real-time dashboards, revenue and P&L reporting |
| **AI operations** | AI agents & customer-service chatbots, an internal AI gateway, automation with human approval and audit trails |

**Products & tooling:** ZaloCRM (multi-account Zalo management + AI customer service, PWA) · DigiHub (connector & data-sync layer for Vietnamese SMEs).

## Why Diginno

- **Deep Lark/LarkBase specialization** for operations and data design
- **n8n at production scale** — many workflows, many systems, monitored
- **Business-first** — process, KPIs, and operating reality before tools
- **Production mindset** — approvals, logs, permissions, handover, and maintainability from day one
- **Built from real delivery**, not internal experiments
- **Long-term partnership** — implement → train → operate → keep improving

## How we work

1. Understand the business process first
2. Design the data model and workflow
3. Implement automation and integrations
4. Add AI where it creates real operational value
5. Support rollout, training, and ongoing operation

## Notes on public work

Many client implementations remain private because they contain internal workflows and business data. Public technical showcases are shared selectively.
